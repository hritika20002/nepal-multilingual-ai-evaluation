# Evaluation Rubric

Score each criterion from **1 to 5**.

## Instruction Adherence
1 = ignores the task or major constraints  
3 = follows the main request but misses some constraints  
5 = fully follows the task and all stated constraints  

## Factual Consistency
1 = contains major factual errors  
3 = mostly accurate with minor uncertainty/errors  
5 = accurate and internally consistent  

## Language Quality
1 = difficult to understand or seriously unnatural  
3 = understandable with noticeable awkwardness  
5 = clear, natural, grammatically strong language  

## Contextual Appropriateness
1 = context is misunderstood or culturally inappropriate  
3 = generally appropriate but generic  
5 = strongly relevant to the language and context  

## Completeness
1 = substantially incomplete  
3 = answers the main request but misses useful details  
5 = complete without unnecessary content  

## Hallucination
Use `Yes` when the response invents unsupported facts, names, statistics, sources, or details.
Use `No` otherwise.

## Overall Quality
1 = unusable  
2 = poor  
3 = acceptable  
4 = good  
5 = excellent  

## Suggested Error Categories
- none
- instruction_failure
- factual_error
- hallucination
- translation_error
- language_quality
- incomplete_answer
- contextual_error
- formatting_error
- other
